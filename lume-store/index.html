Your questions, answered

How long until I see results?

Most people notice conditioned, softer lashes within 2 weeks and a visibly fuller appearance around 6–8 weeks of nightly use. Consistency is key.

How do I use it?

Once nightly on clean, dry skin: swipe along the upper lash line like eyeliner, and brush through your brows. Let it absorb before bed.

Is it safe for sensitive eyes and contact lenses?

It's formulated to be gentle, vegan, and free from parabens and sulfates. As with any eye-area product, patch test first and remove contacts before applying.
